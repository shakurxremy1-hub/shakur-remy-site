Helotes

A Hill Country feel on the northwest edge of San Antonio — established and newer neighborhoods, Northside ISD schools, and open space at the doorstep.

Typical range $320K–$900K Location Northwest · Loop 1604 & Bandera Rd Schools Northside ISD Vibe Semi-rural, established

The overview

Helotes runs along Bandera Road (SH 16) just outside Loop 1604 on the northwest side, where the city grid gives way to hills and oak. It keeps a small-town identity — the Old Town district, the John T. Floore Country Store, an annual cornyval — while sitting minutes from the retail and job centers of far northwest San Antonio.

Government Canyon State Natural Area and the surrounding topography give the area its character: larger lots, tree cover, and quiet, with a trade-off of a single main road (Bandera) that carries most of the traffic.

The homes

Helotes has a wide range: 1990s and 2000s homes on generous lots in communities like Ranchos del Bandera and Iron Horse Canyon, newer construction in Havenwood and Sundance, and custom and acreage homes tucked into the hills. Sizes run from about 1,800 to 4,500-plus square feet, many with greenbelt or canyon lots and HOAs.

Schools

Helotes is served by Northside ISD, one of the largest districts in the state, with O’Connor and Sotomayor among the high schools serving the area. Campus assignments vary by subdivision, so confirm the elementary, middle, and high school for any exact address.

What to know before you buy or sell

  • Bandera Road is the main artery and backs up at peak times — test your actual commute before you commit.
  • Hillside and canyon lots can involve drainage, retaining walls, and foundation questions; inspect with that in mind.
  • Parts of the area are on or near the Edwards Aquifer recharge zone, which affects some build and drainage rules.
  • Sellers: land, trees, and privacy carry real value here — make sure your listing shows the lot, not just the interior.
