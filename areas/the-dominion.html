The Dominion

San Antonio’s best-known guard-gated golf community — estate lots, a private club, and round-the-clock security off I-10.

Typical range $700K–$6M+ Location I-10 at Camp Bullis Security 24/7 manned gate Schools Northside ISD

The overview

The Dominion sits on the northwest edge of the city where the hills start, just off Interstate 10 at Camp Bullis Road. It opened in the 1980s around a private country club and has grown into roughly 1,600 homes organized under a master association with more than twenty sub-neighborhoods, each with its own character and rules.

The defining features are the staffed entry gate and patrol, the golf course and club at the center, and large lots with hill-country privacy — all still only about 20 minutes from the airport and the northwest medical and business corridor.

The homes

Architecture leans Mediterranean, Tuscan, and Spanish, with a growing share of contemporary custom builds. Sizes and prices vary a lot by section: patio and villa homes at the entry level, and gated inner enclaves with acre-plus estate parcels, golf frontage, and long views at the top of the range.

Schools & club

Addresses are served by Northside ISD. Country club membership (golf, tennis, dining, fitness) is separate from the HOA — you can own in The Dominion without joining, and membership tiers and initiation are handled directly with the club.

What to know before you buy or sell

  • There is a master HOA plus a sub-association for most streets — get both fee schedules, resale certificates, and rules early.
  • Showings require gate clearance and an appointment; plan lead time.
  • Some sections have architectural-control committees that review exterior changes — factor that into renovation plans.
  • Sellers: presentation and accurate positioning against the right sub-neighborhood comps matter more here than almost anywhere in the city.
