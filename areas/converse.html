Converse

Affordable, fast-growing northeast San Antonio — newer subdivisions, strong starter-home inventory, and a short drive to Randolph AFB.

Typical range $200K–$340K Location Northeast · near Loop 1604 Schools Judson ISD Vibe Newer, value-driven

The overview

Converse is a suburban city just northeast of San Antonio, wrapped by Loop 1604 with quick connections to I-10, I-35, and FM 78. It has grown quickly over the last two decades as builders opened new subdivisions on former ranch land, and it’s now one of the most active parts of the metro for buyers looking for a newer home under the city’s median price.

The draw is simple: more house for the money, newer construction, and proximity to Randolph Air Force Base, which makes it a steady market for military and first-time buyers using VA and FHA financing.

The homes

Most inventory is one- and two-story brick-and-stone tract homes from the 2000s onward, roughly 1,400 to 2,800 square feet, on modest lots with an HOA in the newer sections. You’ll also find pockets of older, larger-lot homes closer to downtown Converse. New construction is still being delivered on the edges of town.

Schools

Converse is served primarily by Judson ISD, with some addresses in Schertz-Cibolo-Universal City ISD depending on the subdivision. Campus assignments vary block to block, so confirm the exact elementary, middle, and high school for any specific address.

What to know before you buy or sell

  • Confirm the school district and campuses for the exact address — the Judson / SCUCISD line runs through this area.
  • Newer subdivisions carry an HOA; read the dues, rules, and any transfer fees before you’re under contract.
  • Some sections sit near flight paths for Randolph AFB — visit at different times of day if noise matters to you.
  • Sellers: this is a high-supply, comparison-heavy market. Pricing tight to the closest recent sales in your own subdivision is what moves it.
