# listedbyremy.com — Newsletter system

**Name:** The San Antonio Market Note
**From:** Shakur Remy, REALTOR® · Relive Realty
**Cadence:** monthly, 1st week of the month
**Goes to:** past clients + sphere of influence + everyone who signs up on listedbyremy.com
**Job:** stay top-of-mind so you get the referral and the repeat client. A monthly
note to 200 people is worth more than any ad — the people who already trust you
are the ones who send business.

## How to send it (free)

1. **MailerLite** (free up to 1,000 subscribers) — mailerlite.com. Import your
   contacts, paste the issue below, schedule.
2. Point the listedbyremy.com newsletter form at MailerLite (it has a form
   embed) so signups flow straight in. Or keep FormSubmit and add new signups
   by hand each month — fine under ~300.
3. Send from `sremy@reliverealty.com`. Same time every month (e.g. first Tuesday, 9am).

## Format (every issue)

1. **The number that matters** — one stat, one sentence on what it means
2. **If you're buying** — 2–3 sentences
3. **If you're selling** — 2–3 sentences
4. **Neighborhood spotlight** — rotate: Converse, Schertz/Cibolo, Alamo Ranch, Stone Oak, Alamo Heights, The Dominion
5. **One practical tip** — a lender term, a prep step, a tax date
6. **Ask** — reply with a question / free home value / know someone moving?

Keep it under 300 words. People skim.
