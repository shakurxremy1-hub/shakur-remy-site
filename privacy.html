Privacy

Updated November 10, 2026

What this site collects

Only what you choose to send. If you fill in the contact form, the home valuation form or the free checklist form, I receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, what you are interested in, and any message or property address you add.

How I use it

To reply to you, send what you asked for (for example the checklist or a welcome email), and help you buy or sell a home. I do not sell your information. I do not share it with anyone except the services that help me deliver it, listed below.

Services that handle your information

  • Form delivery: Web3Forms passes your form entry to me by email.
  • Email: Google (Gmail) sends the welcome email and receives replies.
  • Instant alerts: Twilio relays a notification to me when a form comes in.
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages hosts this site and, like any host, may log technical details such as your IP address.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts delivers the typefaces and receives your IP address when they load.
  • Optional video chat: if you open the “Chat with Remy” assistant, a third-party video provider handles the session, and your browser asks permission before using your camera or microphone.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently run advertising trackers or analytics. If that changes, I will update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Keeping records

I keep inquiries for as long as needed to help you and to meet record-keeping rules that apply to Texas real estate licensees.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete what you sent, or to stop email from me, by writing to sremy@reliverealty.com. Every email I send you can be answered with “stop”.

Children

This site is for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Other websites

Links to listing sites, maps, Calendly and similar services take you off this site, and their own privacy policies apply there.
