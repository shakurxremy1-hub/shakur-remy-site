# FSBO Playbook — San Antonio

FSBO (For Sale By Owner) sellers have **publicly signaled they want to sell** and
listed their own contact info to do it. Reaching out is standard, defensible
practice — done right. This is the reusable part: scripts, cadence, compliance.

**Honest note:** I can't hand you a ready-to-call list. FSBO sites (Zillow, fsbo.com,
ForSaleByOwner) gate the seller's phone number behind their platform or a paid
feed, and cold-calling requires a DNC scrub first. You pull the list; this playbook
is what you do with each one.

---

| Source | How |
|---|---|
| **Zillow** | zillow.com/san-antonio-tx/fsbo/ — filter "For Sale By Owner". ~90 listings. Contact goes through Zillow's relay unless the owner posted a number in the description. |
| **fsbo.com** | fsbo.com/search/list/san-antonio-tx — ~60 listings, some show a phone on the detail page. |
| **ForSaleByOwner.com** | forsalebyowner.com/search/list/san-antonio-texas |
| **Facebook Marketplace** | search "house for sale by owner San Antonio" — filter Properties. Message through Messenger. |
| **Craigslist** | sanantonio.craigslist.org → housing → real estate - by owner. Numbers often in the post body. |
| **Yard signs** | driving your farm area — the highest-intent FSBOs never list online. |

**If you want volume:** agents use **REDX** (~$60/mo) or **Landvoice** — they
pull FSBO + expired listings daily *with* phone numbers *and* DNC-scrub them. That's
the tool for this to be a real channel. Everything below still applies.

## Compliance checklist (run every time)

- [ ] **Call hours only:** 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM their time. No exceptions.
- [ ] **Identify yourself** as a licensed agent on the first call. Never imply you're a buyer.
- [ ] **Don't claim you have a buyer** for their home unless you actually do.
- [ ] If they say *"take me off your list / don't call again"* — stop immediately, note it, never contact again.
- [ ] **DNC:** if you're pulling numbers yourself, check the national registry (donotcall.gov) or use a tool that scrubs. There's a limited exception for soliciting someone actively marketing their property, but honoring an opt-out is non-negotiable.
- [ ] **TREC:** no misrepresentation in anything you say or send. Your license #844622 and brokerage go in every written follow-up.

## Call 1 — the opener

> "Hi, is this [name]? This is Shakur Remy — I'm a REALTOR® here in San Antonio with
> Relive Realty. I saw you're selling your place on [street] yourself.
>
> I'm not calling to list it out from under you. I work with buyers, and I wanted
> to ask two quick things — is now an okay time?
>
> **1.** If I bring you a qualified, pre-approved buyer, are you open to paying a
> commission on that side only?
>
> **2.** And just so I understand your plan — if it hasn't sold in, say, 3–4 weeks,
> what's your backup? Would it be crazy to have a quick conversation about what a
> full marketing plan would look like, no obligation?"

**If yes to a meeting:** "Great — I can come by [day] at [time] or [day] at [time].
Fifteen minutes, I'll bring recent comps for your street so it's useful either way."

## Objection responses

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I'm not paying any commission." | "Understood. Would you cooperate on the *buyer's* side only if I bring you a ready buyer? You still net more than most listed sales, and you keep control of your side." |
| "I've got it handled." | "That's great — a lot of FSBOs do sell. Can I ask what you're at now, showings-wise? … If it slows down, I'd just want to be the first call. Fair?" |
| "Agents keep calling me." | "I'm sure. I'll make it worth the two minutes: I'll text you three comps for your street right now, no strings. If they're useful, we talk. If not, I won't call again." |
| "Send me something in writing." | "Will do — I'll text you the comps and a one-page plan in the next hour." *(Then send the text below.)* |
| "How much do you charge?" | "It's negotiable and we'd set it before I do anything — but this call is about whether I can help you sell, not about hiring me." |

## Follow-up cadence

**Right after Call 1 (text):**
> Hi [name], Shakur Remy (Relive Realty) — good talking just now. Here are 3 recent
> sales on/near [street]: [links]. When you have 15 min this week I'll bring a plan
> for getting more buyers through the door. — 210-871-1562

**Day 3 (text, if no meeting yet):**
> [name] — any movement on showings this week? If it's quiet, that's usually a
> pricing or exposure fix, not a "wait longer" thing. Worth a quick look?

**Day 7 (email):**
> **Subject:** [street] — a marketing plan, no obligation
>
> Hi [name],
>
> Whether you sell it yourself or list it, you deserve a real plan for getting
> buyers in. Here's what I'd do with your home — pricing read, photos, MLS + syndication,
> open house schedule, and how buyer's agents get paid post-2024:
>
> [1-page plan attached or bulleted]
>
> If it's useful, I'm around this week. If you've got it handled, no problem — I'll
> stop here.
>
> Shakur Remy · REALTOR®, Relive Real Estate Inc. · TREC #844622
> 210-871-1562 · sremy@reliverealty.com · listedbyremy.com

**Day 14:** market update text — "[area] had X new listings / Y sales this week."

**Day 30:** "Still on the market? Let's talk." Most FSBOs list with an agent
between weeks 3 and 8 — be the one who stayed in touch.

## The math
~20 FSBOs worked properly → 3–5 listing appointments → 1–2 listings. FSBOs that
don't sell almost all end up listing with *someone*. Stay in the follow-up and
that someone is you.
