# listedbyremy.com — Client Launch Pack

Everything below is written to **copy, paste, send**. The site and the dashboard are
done. Getting real clients now is outreach you send from your own accounts — this
file is every message pre-written so that part takes minutes, not hours.

Order of impact for the first 30 days:
1. Sphere-of-influence blast (below) — your warm list is the fastest closing
2. Google Business Profile — free, ranks you in "realtor near me"
3. Agent profiles on Zillow / Realtor.com / Homes.com — free inbound
4. One open house every weekend + the follow-up sequence
5. Two referral partners (a lender + a title rep) feeding you weekly

---

## 4. Open house — plan + follow-up

You don't need your own listing. Ask agents in your brokerage to host theirs.
One per weekend.

**At the door:** every visitor signs in with name + phone + email + "are you
working with an agent?" (paper sheet or a Google Form on a tablet).

**Follow-up sequence** — send to every non-agent visitor:

*Same day (text):*
> Hi [Name] — Shakur here, hosted the open house on [Street] today. Great meeting
> you. If you want the disclosures or comps for that one, or want to see anything
> else this week, just say the word.

*Day 2 (email):*
> **Subject:** [Street] — plus 3 similar you might like
>
> Hi [Name], enjoyed talking today. I pulled three homes in the same
> area and price range — links below. If any are worth a look I can get us in
> this week. And if the timing's longer than that, no problem: tell me the
> criteria and I'll set up a search that emails you only the ones that actually
> fit.
>
> [3 links]
>
> Shakur · 646-688-3442 · listedbyremy.com

*Day 7 (text):*
> [Name] — anything jump out from that list I sent? Happy to line up showings for
> the weekend if so.

*Day 30 (email):* short market update for that neighborhood + "still thinking
about a move?"

## 5. Referral partners (recurring pipeline)

You want **one lender** and **one title/escrow rep** who send you buyers/sellers
and to whom you send business back. Coffee, then a standing monthly check-in.

**Outreach to a loan officer (LinkedIn DM or email):**

> Hi [Name] — I'm a REALTOR® with Relive Realty here in San Antonio, building my
> business the right way: referral relationships over ad spend. I'm looking for
> one lender partner I can send every pre-approval to and trust completely. Could
> we grab 20 minutes this week? I'd rather build this with someone local who
> actually picks up the phone on a Saturday.

**Outreach to a title/escrow rep:**

> Hi [Name] — Shakur Remy, REALTOR® with Relive Realty. I'm setting up my
> preferred vendors and would like to meet the title rep I'll be sending my
> closings to. 15–20 minutes this week? I bring the files organized and I don't
> disappear between contract and close.

## 6. First 10 posts (content that brings inbound)

Post 3×/week. Value first, ask last.

1. "I'm a REALTOR® now" announcement (post from section 1)
2. **Neighborhood spotlight:** Converse — what $250–320K buys right now, 3 photos
3. **First-time buyer myth:** "you need 20% down" — walk through 3% conventional / 0 down VA
4. **This week's San Antonio numbers:** median price, days on market, months of inventory (pull from MLS)
5. **Behind the scenes:** a showing day, 4 quick clips, "what I look for that buyers miss"
6. **Seller tip:** the 5 cheap things that add the most at listing (paint, light, declutter, landscaping, photos)
7. **Neighborhood spotlight:** Stone Oak vs. Alamo Ranch — schools, commute, price, who each fits
8. **Client win:** a closing photo (with permission) + one sentence on the hard part you solved
9. **Explainer:** what closing costs actually are, line by line, for a $300K SA home
10. **Direct ask:** "Three people I talk to this month will buy or sell this year. Comment or DM 🏡 and let's talk timeline."

Every post ends with: *listedbyremy.com*.

## 7. Expired / FSBO calls (optional, highest effort / highest reward)

Pull expired listings and FSBOs from the MLS / Zillow "For sale by owner." Call
within 24–48h.

**FSBO opener:**
> Hi, this is Shakur Remy with Relive Realty — I saw you're selling [Address]
> yourself. I'm not calling to list it out from under you. I work with buyers, and
> I wanted to ask: if I bring you a qualified buyer, are you willing to cooperate
> on a commission for that side only?
> *(Then:)* And just so I know — if it hasn't sold in a few weeks, would it be
> crazy to have a backup conversation about what a full marketing plan looks like?

**Expired opener:**
> Hi [Name], Shakur Remy with Relive Realty. I noticed your home at [Address] came
> off the market. I'm not going to pretend I know exactly why — but I've looked at
> the listing and I have a few specific ideas about pricing and marketing that I
> think were missing. Could I come by for 15 minutes and show you what I'd do
> differently? No obligation.
