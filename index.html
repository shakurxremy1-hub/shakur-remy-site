New to this

Buying your first home in San Antonio.

Most people I work with aren’t buying luxury. They’re buying their first place, and they carry the same three questions everyone does. Here are the straight answers, no jargon.

“Do I need 20% down?”

No. In this market most first-time buyers I work with put down far less.

  • FHA loans start around 3.5% down
  • VA and USDA can be 0% down if you qualify
  • Texas has down-payment assistance and first-time buyer programs
  • We’ll get you with a lender who’ll lay out your real number for free

“Is my credit good enough?”

Often yes. And if it’s not quite there yet, we build a plan to get you there.

  • Many loan programs start in the low-600s
  • A lender pull tells us exactly where you stand, no guessing
  • If you’re a few months out, I’ll tell you what to fix first
  • No pressure to buy before you’re ready

“What does the process even look like?”

Five steps, and I’m with you on every one.

  • Talk it through: budget, neighborhoods, timeline
  • Get pre-approved so we know your range
  • Tour homes on your schedule
  • Offer, option period, inspection
  • Close, usually 30–45 days, keys in hand

Good to know

Questions buyers and sellers ask.

How is my agent paid when I buy?

Since 2024, buyers sign a written representation agreement that states the agent’s compensation before touring homes. That fee is negotiable and may be paid by the seller, by you, or split between you. We go over the numbers up front, so there are no surprises at closing.

What is the Texas option period?

For a small option fee, the buyer gets a set number of days to terminate the contract for any reason. It’s when inspections happen. If anything material turns up, you can renegotiate or walk and get your earnest money back.

How much earnest money do I need?

In the San Antonio market it’s commonly around 1% of the purchase price, held by the title company and credited to you at closing. Stronger earnest money can make an offer more competitive on a sought-after home.

What does it cost to sell?

Typically: negotiated agent compensation, title and escrow fees, prorated property taxes, and sometimes a survey, HOA transfer, or buyer concessions. I give you a net-proceeds estimate before we list and update it with every offer.

Do I need to be pre-approved before we start touring?

It's the right first step for any budget. A lender pre-approval tells us your real price range, and it lets us move fast and write a strong offer when the right home shows up. Some higher-priced listings require it before a showing is even confirmed.

How long does a purchase take?

About 30–45 days from accepted offer to closing when financed, faster for cash. New construction runs on the builder’s timeline.

What are property taxes like here?

Texas has no state income tax; property tax rates in Bexar County generally run a little over 2% of assessed value depending on the taxing entities. A homestead exemption lowers the taxable value on your primary residence.

Can you represent me on a new-construction home?

Yes, and it’s worth having representation. The builder’s contract is written to protect the builder, not you, so I review the agreement, incentives, upgrades, and timeline before you sign anything.
